## Why there are two parts

The roadmap's roll is `202610` → `202611`. On 2026-09-27, step 1's
`rollover` conformance case had already closed `202609` on both boards and
put the leftovers in `202610`. Jira's `202610` was still a future sprint
that nothing had started, so part 1 asked the question the calendar raises
on Oct 1 (`202609` → `202610`), and part 2 is the gate's roll.

Plane's part 2 waited until Oct 2. Plane completes a cycle only from the
day after it starts, and the adapter counts days on this machine's clock.

| 5 | In both part 2s, the re-read of the old iteration went out together with `create_iteration`, not after it. | None needed. The re-read has to come before the move, and it did; creating an iteration touches no items. |
| 6 | Plane's by-key read reports no iteration. `GET /workspaces/…/work-items/AGADMIN-2/` returns `cycle_id: null`, while the by-id read and the item list carry it. The fake transport has every read carry it. | Not on the gate's path: `plan-sprint` reads an iteration's items through its own list, and its backlog filter uses the item list. A follow-up for the adapter and the fake. |
| 7 | Both adapters take "today" from the machine's local date, but read iteration dates in the board's own time zone. On the evening of Oct 1 (CDT), it was already Oct 2 in UTC while the adapter still counted Oct 1. That's read from the code, not seen as a live refusal. | A follow-up: count today in the board's time zone. |
